01 / CAMPUS DNA

A university designed around connection, not separation.

XJTLU Entrepreneur College (Taicang) explores a different model of higher education through Syntegrative Education. Students, academics, industry and society are brought into the same ecosystem rather than existing in isolated spaces.

02 / ARCHITECTURE

The circle is the idea.

The Taicang campus is organised around an extraordinary circular structure connecting its industry-themed schools.

Instead of treating disciplines as separate islands, the campus creates physical connections between different fields, people and ideas.

The architecture therefore becomes more than a collection of buildings. It becomes a visual expression of the College's philosophy: connection creates possibility.
